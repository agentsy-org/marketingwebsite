CASE STUDY 01

Real Estate. Rebuilt Around AI.

Web + native iOS AI platform

The opportunity was bigger than generating property descriptions.

What if AI became part of the entire real estate workflow?

Agentsy designed and developed a web and native iOS platform that used AI across:

  • Property marketing.
  • Listings.
  • Content.
  • Virtual styling.
  • Reporting.
  • Automation.
  • Operational workflows.

The objective was simple:

Give agents better tools and less busywork.

The result was a purpose-built AI experience designed around how real estate professionals actually work.

The Agentsy Advantage

Don’t add AI to the edge of the business. Design the workflow around what’s now possible.
